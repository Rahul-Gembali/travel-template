# HORIZONS — Brag Launch Video Plan

## 1. Planning Rubric

1. **What is the product?**
   HORIZONS is a slow travel web application and living digital monograph designed for mindful travelers who reject the frantic checklist mentality of tourism. It pairs architectural aesthetics with curated slow stays, seasonal weather telemetry, artisan guides, and mindful cultural journeys across Kyoto, Oaxaca, Tbilisi, Lisbon, Cape Town, and Bergen.
   Live URL: `https://rahul-gembali.github.io/travel-template/`

2. **Who is the audience?**
   Designers, architects, creative technologists, independent travelers, and developers who appreciate editorial typography, tactile design systems, analog-inspired craftsmanship, and high-performance web engineering.

3. **What is the core hook?**
   Rejecting the sterile, cookie-cutter look of modern SaaS and AI-slop travel sites by demonstrating that travel begins with contemplation: translating digital UI into the analog physics of a master artist drawing with a 2H–9B graphite pencil on 640gsm cold-press cotton rag paper.

4. **What tone fits best?**
   **Architectural Monograph & Sumi-e Meditation**: Unhurried, deeply tactile, editorial, and confident. Drawing heavily from both *Neo-Minimal 2D/3D hybrid* motion and Japanese *Sumi-e (墨絵)* philosophy where the unpainted space (*Ma*) breathes and speaks as powerfully as the marks themselves.

5. **What format and duration?**
   16:9 Landscape (1920×1080), 24 fps, exactly 20.0 seconds (480 frames).

6. **What are the visual chapters?**
   - Beat 1 (0.0s – 3.5s | Frames 0–84): The Blank Cotton Sheet & 2H Contour Wireframe
   - Beat 2 (3.5s – 7.5s | Frames 85–180): 4B Cross-Hatching & Greasy 8B Volumetric Arch
   - Beat 3 (7.5s – 14.5s | Frames 181–348): Hand-Drawn Journey across 3 Archetypes (Kyoto, Oaxaca, Bergen)
   - Beat 4 (14.5s – 17.5s | Frames 349–420): Dynamic Obsidian Slate Inversion & Monograph Climax
   - Beat 5 (17.5s – 20.0s | Frames 421–480): Tortillon Blending Stump Smudge & Vermillion Hanko Seal

7. **How does motion behave?**
   Governed by the 70% deceleration settle rule from `/motion-graphics`: elements accelerate with purpose and spend the majority of their travel decelerating gracefully into place with damped harmonic spring physics (`stiffness: 120`, `damping: 14`). Camera drifts continuously on a subtle physical arc (`hand: 1.5`).

8. **How does the audio work?**
   100% procedural ASMR foley generated via the Web Audio API without external audio files:
   - Dual-pole pink noise bandpass filter simulating pencil lead dragging across cotton fibers
   - Tactile cedarwood pencil taps at title and scene locks
   - Resonant 45Hz sub-bass sine impact during the obsidian slate inversion
   - Soft whisper of paper friction during the tortillon blending pass

9. **What is the final call to action?**
   The live deployment URL `rahul-gembali.github.io/travel-template/` revealed beneath smudged graphite and stamped with an authentic vermillion artist seal (*Hanko* — `#C26D53`).
