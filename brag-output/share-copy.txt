## LinkedIn Post
In an era dominated by rapid-fire tourism and homogenized digital experiences, we wanted to build something that honors stillness, craftsmanship, and architectural restraint.

Introducing HORIZONS: A Slow Travel Guide & Living Digital Monograph.

Key Design & Engineering Principles:
• Substrate Tactility: An analog design system inspired by 640gsm cold-press cotton rag paper and 2H–9B graphite drafting.
• Sumi-e (墨絵) Philosophy: Embracing Ma (sacred negative space), intentional pacing, and wabi-sabi imperfections.
• Living Telemetry: Real-time seasonal weather metrics, pace dials, and quiet retreat curation across six global citadels.
• Production Performance: Zero external bloated frameworks, 60fps buttery smooth transitions, and seamless mobile responsiveness.
